# RP5 art list — the 126 photographs the last twelve books need

Written in Session RP5, after every one of those books got its richer layout.

Companion to `kidspedia-rp5-prompts.md`, which is what gets pasted into ChatGPT.

**Two piles.** Six books (Sharks, Dinosaurs, The Moon, Big Cats, Penguins, Bugs &
Butterflies) are LIVE and already have a cover and four page photos — they need only
their fact photos. The other six (Snakes & Reptiles, The Planets, Rockets & Astronauts,
Volcanoes, Wild Weather, The Deep Ocean) have no photographs at all and stay
`in-review` until the whole set lands.

**Sizing (RP2/RP4, learned the hard way).** Fact photos 1200px on the long edge, WebP q80.
Square and Then/Now slots 800px q74 — they render about 128px across, so anything bigger
is weight for nothing. Standing slots 1000px q80. Cover and page photos 1600px q80.
**Crop for the slot BEFORE converting** — a round or polaroid slot centre-crops and will
not go hunting for the subject.
