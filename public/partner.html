How it works

Imagination in. A real creation out.

We don't ask AI to write risky new code every time. Kids' ideas plug into pre-built, tested game engines and art libraries — so every creation is safe, reliable, and instantly playable.

1

Kid describes an idea

In their own words or voice — a fairy garden, a space race, a song about bees.

2

AI + our engines build it

The idea drops into pre-built engines and reusable art libraries — no fragile one-off code.

3

They play it in seconds

Instantly playable, always winnable, and theirs to share, remix, and grow.
