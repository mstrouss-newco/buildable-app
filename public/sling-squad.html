How to play

Drag an animal back in the slingshot, aim, and let go to fling them!

Each animal has a super power — tap the screen while they fly to use it! Splitz splits into 3, Boomer goes BOOM, Bruno smashes down heavy, and Zip zooms super fast.

Knock over every silly block tower and bonk all the goofy targets until they go POOF. Clear them all before you run out of slings — but don't worry, you get plenty of tries.
