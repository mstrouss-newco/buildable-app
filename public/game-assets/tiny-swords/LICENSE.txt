How we comply (per ASSET-LIBRARY.md):
- We CURATE only the specific sprites Castle Guard uses into this folder and
  serve them from our own domain inside the game. We do NOT expose a browsable
  or downloadable copy of the whole pack.
- Goblin enemies are in the PAID "Enemy Pack" and are NOT in the free pack, so
  Castle Guard's silly baddies reskin the free RED "Pawn" unit (decision with
  owner, 2026-06-28).

Curated files (all from the Free Pack):
  archer_idle/shoot, arrow        <- Blue Archer (defender)  -> theme: castle
  baddie_idle/run                 <- Red Pawn (silly baddie)
  castle, tower                   <- Blue Buildings
  tree, bush1, bush2, rock1, rock2 <- Terrain/Decorations
