Games on your terms.

Feel a little less bad about screen time. Every game you build can carry a few grown-up rules that kids barely notice.

Vegetables before dessert

Sneak in math, spelling, or typing. The cannon only fires when they solve 7 x 8. The dragon needs a word spelled right before it flies. We built Number Cannon and Minute Math this way, and our kids ask to play them.

Play unlocks afterHomeworkFeed the dogReading 20 min

Chores first, then the game

Set a short list. The game opens when it's checked off. No arguing with you, only the game, and the game doesn't budge.

Today's play time30 min

You set the clock

Pick how long. When time is up the game wraps the level and says goodnight, without a countdown nagging them to come back tomorrow.

The cool-parent moment

Send one link. It opens on any phone, no app, no account. Watch the cousins fight over the high score in a game your kid invented and you built.

Questions parents ask

Do I need to know anything about coding or games?

No. If you can text, you can build. You make choices, we make the game.

What ages does this work for?

Kids 4 to 12 love building. Little ones point and pick, older kids take over the controls.

Can grandma play without signing up?

Yes. The link opens in any phone browser. No app, no account.

Is my kid's stuff safe?

Games only go where you send them. No public profile, no chat, no messages from anyone.

What happens when I hit my monthly games?

You can still play and change every game you have. Add more for $5 when you need them.
