The problem

Most screen time is watch, tap, repeat.
And it's lonely.

Kids are handed apps that entertain them but ask nothing of them. And parents are left worrying about the same three things.

Passive

They watch and tap. They rarely make anything of their own.

Unsafe

Open chat and strangers are one wrong tap away.

Empty

Hours go by, and parents wonder if any of it taught their kid a thing.

For parents & educators

Built for their imagination.
Designed for your peace of mind.

Buildable is not an open internet playground. It's a structured creative space where kids build, not browse. No open chat, no rabbit holes. Just creation — guided, safe, and age-appropriate.

🛡️
Guided creation, not open chat
Kids bring their own ideas to life in a guided space. There's no freeform conversation, no access to the open web, no surprises.
📊
Weekly progress reports
A weekly email digest plus a live skills dashboard: every subject practiced, every skill earned, in every game they play.
🎚️
You set the level
Set each child's grade level and switch the learning layer on or off, per kid, anytime. Difficulty adapts as they grow.
✅
You review before they publish
Nothing goes public without your approval. Kids keep building; you stay in control of what the world sees.
