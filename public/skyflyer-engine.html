How to fly

Drag anywhere on the screen to steer. Drag up and down too. Your ride always flies forward by itself.

Pick which ride you fly in the hangar, on the Make it mine screen. Every ride scoops the same coins and lands on the same pads - they just look and feel different.

Scoop up gold coins in the sky. Bumping into things just gives you a soft bounce, so you can never crash.

Follow the orange arrow to a landing pad. Fly low over it to land and bank your coins.
