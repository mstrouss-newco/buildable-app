Tile Shots — contact sheet

Every game tile could show a real picture of the game instead of an AI painting. These are the first two, taken by the games themselves. Nothing on the live site has changed. Say which look you want and the rest of the games follow.

Space Survival

The dark world. Sunset sky, the jetpack hero, three cloud foes, one sparkle mid-flight, one coin.

Space Survival

How it reads at the size a tile is really seen. The card, the colour dot and the name do not change — only the picture behind them.

Castle Guard

The bright world. The castle on the right, two archers, three goblins on the road, one arrow mid-flight.

Castle Guard

Green on green: the wash barely shows here, which is worth knowing before eighteen more games get one.

Three things to decide

  • Wash or no wash. On Space Survival the purple reads nicely. On Castle Guard the green all but disappears, because the grass is already that colour.
  • Is a real screenshot better than the painting? The photo is honest about what the game looks like. The painting is prettier and less true.
  • Castle Guard has no coin on the field, so it has no treat in the picture. Tower defence keeps its coins in the info bar, which the photo hides.
