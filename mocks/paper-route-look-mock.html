Paper Route, painted

You said the shipped ride looked flat and cheap next to Hop Heroes, and picked the full watercolor route. This is that, in two halves: what the game already looks like today after the first half, and what the second half puts on top of it. Nothing is in front of a kid yet. Paper Route stays behind the coming soon gate until you say this is right.

Half one: the street is painted, not filled in

This half is already done and it needs no new pictures at all. The game does the painting itself, every frame. Real shots from a real phone below.

What changed, in order of how much it matters

  • The far end melts away. A band of haze sits on the horizon, so the road and the last houses fade into the distance instead of stopping on a hard line. This is the single biggest thing that stopped it reading as clip art.
  • Nothing floats. Every house, tree, bush, mailbox, bin, cone, car, van and the bike itself now sits in a soft pool of shade.
  • The sky is lit. One warm sun high on the right, and everything below it is warmed to match.
  • The road has a surface. A warm crown of light down the middle where the sun catches the camber, cooler and darker as it goes away, a scatter of speckle for tooth, and warm cream kerbs with a line of shade where they meet the grass.
  • The grass is a lawn. Three greens instead of one, brushed over with patches and little dabs of flowers that scroll past with the street.
  • Boosting feels fast. The whole picture smears and speed lines stream out of the distance, instead of four blue sticks behind the wheel.

All of it is drawn by the game as it plays. No extra downloads, nothing new for a kid's iPad to wait for.

Half two: real watercolor pieces

The houses, trees, bike and vans in the shots above are still the flat shapes from last session. Half two replaces every one of them with a painted cut-out from the same picture library Hop Heroes uses, so Paper Route is on the house style rather than near it. The plumbing is built and the library is registered; the pieces get painted the moment this goes live.

Three houses rather than two, so a row of doors does not repeat every other house. The rider, the car and the van are painted from behind, because the camera rides over the kid's shoulder.

Each one is painted the same way every other piece in the library is: a single thing, on its own, on see-through backing, nothing else in the frame. Painted once, kept forever, about a penny each. Every piece is also filed in the shared library tagged suburb, town and beach, so the next game that wants a street gets one for free.

If a picture ever fails to arrive

The street you see in the AFTER shots is still underneath all of it. A slow phone, a missed picture or an outage puts the drawn version back, and the ride carries on. There is no state where Paper Route is broken because a painting did not load.
