# Kidspedia RP4 Prompt Pack — the 56 fact photos for the seven converted books

Companion to `kidspedia-rp4-art-list.md`, which says what each slot is FOR.
This file is what actually gets pasted into ChatGPT, one prompt at a time.

## How to run it

1. **One prompt per message.** Never paste two — that produces a collage.
2. Start every prompt with `Generate a single image (not a grid or collage):`
   then the STYLE LINE for the book, then the SHAPE LINE, then the subject.
3. Fetch the blob in the page under the exact filename (see the TB5 download
   recipe in the `kidspedia-topic-book-plan` memory). Do not use the share icon.
4. `bk-` prefix on every filename so these stay separate from Mike's own downloads.

## The style line (all 20 books look like one set)

> Ultra-realistic professional documentary photograph, warm natural light, crisp
> detail, kid-friendly and gentle (nothing scary, no blood), no text, no words,
> no watermark.

## The shape lines (the slot centre-crops, so shape matters more than anything)

| Slot shape in the art list | Shape line to append |
|---|---|
| SQUARE (circle or Then/Now slot) | `Square format, 1:1. The subject is dead centre and fills the frame.` |
| PORTRAIT 3:4 | `Tall portrait format. One standing subject, centred, head to foot in frame.` |
| NEAR-SQUARE 4:3 (polaroid) | `Square format, 1:1. The subject fills the frame — a polaroid crop will cut the edges.` |
| wide 16:10 | `Wide landscape format. Subject centred, plenty of room either side.` |

## The optional chart chips

42 are listed in `kidspedia-rp4-art-list.md`. The bar set in RP2 is that a chip
only beats a drawn glyph when the real thing is more striking AND its background
is pale. Most of these are silhouettes of familiar things (a kid, a door, a
giraffe) where the drawn glyph is already honest, so RP4 ships the photos first
and treats chips as a separate, smaller decision.
