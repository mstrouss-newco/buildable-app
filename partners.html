Passive

What Buildable Kids is

A full creative studio, made just for kids.

Create

Games, music, art & stories

No coding, no blank canvas. Kids start from polished games and creative tools, then customize characters, worlds, art, music, levels, and difficulty. The AI handles the hard parts.

Safe

Safe by design

Play with siblings, parents, grandparents, and approved friends only. Friend connections need approval from both kids' parents. No open chat. No strangers. Ever.

Remix

A marketplace kids fill

Kids publish, play, and remix each other's creations in a moderated marketplace built around creativity, not social networking.

Learn

Learning built in

A learning layer parents and teachers can switch on or off. Kids answer real questions to earn credits they spend on building, so learning powers the fun instead of interrupting it.

The infrastructure

We didn't build 25 games.
We built the machine that makes them.

Every game runs on shared engines, shared art, shared screens, and shared safety. Each new title costs a fraction of the last, and every upgrade improves the whole catalog at once.

Kids create on the same rails we do, and for each other

The same tools we use to make games are in kids' hands. Kids generate art, characters, worlds, and whole games with AI, and what one kid makes becomes building blocks for every other kid. The platform's content supply is the kids themselves.

One engine, every game

Levels are recipes, not code. New games reuse proven building blocks, so the 26th game costs a fraction of the first.

AI art & sound pipeline

Nearly 300 shared art pieces plus AI tools that generate new art, music, and sound on demand. The usual studio bottleneck, automated.

Safe multiplayer, built once

Any game goes online by flipping a switch. Siblings connect automatically; friends need approval from both kids' parents.

Shared screens & upgrades

Menus, loading, navigation, and win screens are built once. One improvement upgrades every game at the same time.

Learning & telemetry

Learning Mode weaves questions into natural pauses in play, while per-kid tracking lays the groundwork for adaptive difficulty and teacher reports.

Robot testers

Automatic testers play through every level on real phone and tablet screens to confirm everything works before it ships.

Most studios get slower as they grow. Ours gets faster, and the kids are the factory too.

The category was just validated

Meta launched Pocket in July 2026: vibe-coded mini games, for adults.

Pocket lets people generate and share mini games from AI prompts, built on Meta's acquisition of the vibe-coding platform Gizmo. It's our exact idea, aimed at grown-ups. But it has no education layer, no kid safety, and it's owned by a social media company parents don't trust with their children. Big Tech just confirmed the behavior. We own the version parents will actually allow.

The game-based learning market was worth roughly $16 to 24 billion in 2024-25 and is projected to reach about $65 billion by 2030, with the school-focused slice alone at about $9.5 billion.

Prodigy is math drills. ABCmouse is watch-and-tap lessons. PBS Kids is shows. Meta built it for adults. We take the proven model and build it for the ones who most want to create: kids.
