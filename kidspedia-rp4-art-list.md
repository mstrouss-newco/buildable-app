# RP4 art list — what the seven converted books still need

Written at the end of Session RP3.

Every fact after the first on a converted page currently shows a DETAIL CROP of that
page's own photo as a stand-in (the template tags them `data-crop` so QA can count
them). Nothing is a white hole, but nothing after the first fact is its own picture
yet. This is the list that finishes them.

**Sizing, learned the hard way in RP2:**

- Fact photos **1200px wide**, WebP quality 80 (about 60-105KB each).
- Chart chips **700px** (about 10-45KB). A chip renders roughly 84px across, so a
  1600px chip is 200KB of nothing.
- **Crop for the slot BEFORE converting.** A round or polaroid slot centre-crops and
  will not go hunting for the subject — a wide maglev shot became a picture of
  concrete in RP2 for exactly this reason.
- Dim historical scenes need brightening (about 1.25 brightness, 1.07 contrast) or
  they are unreadable at chip size.
- Write the caption to the picture you actually got, not the one you imagined.

## Totals

- **56 fact photos — required.** These are what turn a book from stand-ins into
  finished, and they are the whole of RP4.
- **42 chart chips — optional.** A drawn glyph is already honest and already in the
  book's own colour, so a chip only wins where the real thing is more striking than a
  silhouette AND its background is pale. RP2 shipped four of these for Trains and
  kept all four; that is the bar.

Eight photos per book, four books' worth of pages per sitting, is the rhythm RP2 ran at.
