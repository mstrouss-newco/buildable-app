# Kidspedia RP5 Prompt Pack

One prompt per message, never two — two in one message produces a collage.
Prefix every one with `Generate a single image (not a grid or collage):`, then the
STYLE LINE, then the SHAPE LINE, then the subject. Fetch the blob in the page under the
exact filename (see the TB5 download recipe); do not use the share icon.

## The style line (all 20 books look like one set)

> Ultra-realistic professional documentary photograph, warm natural light, crisp detail, kid-friendly and gentle (nothing scary, no blood), no text, no words, no watermark.
