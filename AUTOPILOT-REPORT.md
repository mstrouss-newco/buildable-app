## Autopilot — 2026-08-15 — card FL12

**Needs you first:** nothing.

**What got built:** Sky Flyer now has SKY TRAILS in every world — two or three lines of rings hanging in the air per stop, chased for the chasing. The first ring stands under a soft beam the moment you arrive; fly through it and the whole trail lights up. Every ring plays one note of the coin chime, pitched up per ring (rate 1.00 → 1.07 → 1.14 → 1.21 → 1.28 for a 5-ring trail), so a full trail is a little rising tune; the last ring adds the sparkle cascade, a coin burst and a sticker kept per kid. Four recipe shapes handle every trail — Ribbon (a gentle S at cruise), Dipper (down to the water and back up), Arch (arches over the tallest real landmark near the anchor — an island, a peak, a mesa), Climb (a staircase up to a view). Missing a ring never fails: the trail sits waiting; come back and it lights straight up. Rings are FAT torus (tube 0.9u, magnet 8.5u) so they still read as hoops from the low close camera when seen edge on, and coloured off the level palette (`world.cap`, or `world.leaf` on Snowy Peaks where the cap is nearly white) so every world reads different for free. Every ring samples ground height at placement so a dipper can never end up buried in a hill. Trails join the mini-map and pin the same way jobs do. QA: passed (`qa-skyflyer.mjs`, all 562 checks including ~40 new FL12 checks — every world places 2-3 trails, every ring stands 6-90u above the ground, flying through fires the rising tune with strictly-rising rates, missing a ring never fails and it can be hit later).

**Calls I made for you:** I picked FAT-and-SOFT for the ring look over thin-and-glowing and the hoop-of-little-flags option, because the traps note is right — from the low close camera a thin torus edge on is an invisible line, and a fat one still reads at every angle at phone size. Change it by editing `TR_RING_TUBE` at the top of the FL12 block in `public/skyflyer-engine.html`. I made the ring COLOUR read `world.cap` for two worlds and `world.leaf` for Snowy Peaks — that world's cap is nearly white and the rings would have vanished against the sky. It's a per-terrain switch (styleBody uses the same shape) and still 100% palette-driven, so a manifest recolour recolours the trails too. I added a tiny additive change to `public/buildable-audio.js`: `BA.sfx` now accepts `opt.rate` (positive number) as an explicit pitch override, backward compatible with every existing caller. That's how the rising tune plays without touching the shared sound catalog — I'd rather grow the shared library once for a small correct primitive than fork a bespoke oscillator. I placed 7 trails total (2 in Sunny Islands, 3 in Snowy Peaks, 2 in Sunset Canyon), and every one of the four shapes appears somewhere in the world so the shapes aren't dead code. Anchors sit outside pad approach zones (they join `JOB_POINTS` so chunks never grow on top of a first ring, same trick landing pads use). Trails run alongside jobs — starting a job doesn't hide trails; you can fly through them mid-mission and the chime plays.

## Autopilot — 2026-08-15 — card FL11

**Needs you first:** nothing.

**What got built:** In Sky Flyer's puffin bird-transform quest, the fish a kid catches and carries home to the chicks used to look like plain cubes in the beak. They are now real fish — the same hand-built model that arcs out of the island lagoons in AR1Q, so both places show one animal. Silvery-blue against the puffin's red belly, sized up so a kid spots them from the air, shiny enough that the specular highlight moves across each fish as the puffin banks, and wiggling in the beak (a tail sweep + a small flop) so they are obviously alive. FL13's jumping-fish reaction can reuse this model without any extra work. QA: passed (`qa-skyflyer.mjs`, all 524 checks, including 8 new FL11 checks).

**Calls I made for you:** I honoured the FL5b law rather than shortcutting — the shape comes from a new `carry:"fish"` field on the puffin quest's cargo recipe, and `buildCargo` dispatches on it just like `styleBody` dispatches on `style`. This means adding a fish carriable to another quest costs one recipe field, no engine change. The other option was to hard-code "if it is the puffin quest, draw fish", which was faster to write and would have quietly poisoned every future quest that wanted the same. I also left the pollen/seed/letter carriables as boxes — the card was scoped to the fish, and every one of those still has a good enough SVG icon on the offer card; a future card can hand any of them a `carry` name and it drops through the same gate. The cargo material is a new phong (shininess 110, mild emissive) rather than the shared HB material, so the shine reads at 30 units up without changing what the lagoon fish look like from close.

## Autopilot — 2026-08-15 — card FL10

**Needs you first:** nothing.

**What got built:** In Sky Flyer, the end-of-quest choice (Do it again / Keep flying) is gone. The reward moment still plays (sticker, coins, DID YOU KNOW), then the card fades on its own after ~4.5s and drops straight back into free flight. A tap anywhere skips the beat. The quest is still standing in the world — playing it again is flying back to its beam and saying yes, exactly like finding it the first time. QA: passed (`qa-skyflyer.mjs`, all 516 checks, including four new FL10 checks).

**Calls I made for you:** The beat is 4500ms (`FACT_BEAT_MS` in `public/skyflyer-engine.html`) — long enough for a grown-up to read the fun fact aloud without the kid getting bored. Change it by editing that constant. I also added a tap-anywhere skip so an impatient kid or grown-up can move on early. And I set `declined[JOB.id]=time` on completion so the offer does not re-pop the instant the card closes if you happen to still be sitting on the pad — you have to fly off and back, which matches the "finding it the first time" framing in the card. jsdom was moved to devDependencies since it is a QA-only dep and does not need to ship in production.
